Program 01 · Including stop it

Project assessment

We talk to the people doing the work rather than only the people reporting on it, because those two accounts of a project are rarely the same. The report gives a plain verdict with the reasoning, and sometimes that verdict is to stop — which is unwelcome, cheaper than continuing, and the reason an outside view is worth paying for.

Program 02 · With an end date

Interim IT management

Interim work has one failure mode: becoming permanent by accident. Every engagement has an agreed end date and a handover deliverable from day one, and we document as we go rather than holding knowledge that makes us hard to replace.

Program 03 · What you own, and owe

Supplier and contract review

Organisations routinely cannot say who holds their domain, their code repository or their administrative credentials until they try to leave a supplier. We inventory all of it with the renewal dates, and flag where you do not own something you assumed you did. Whether a contract term is enforceable is a question for a solicitor.

  • Fixed fee
